## Brand & Style

This design system merges the sensory warmth of gastronomy with high-velocity SaaS precision. Built for restaurateurs, bar operators, and hospitality entrepreneurs, it balances high-conversion urgency with tactile hospitality cues. The visual tone evokes artisanal paper stock, sun-drenched terracotta tiles, and aromatic herbs, grounded by clean technological utility.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Contemporary SaaS** with **Soft Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Generous cream backdrops replace sterile tech whites, avoiding eye fatigue in dim dining rooms or bright patio environments.
- High-saturation terracotta-amber accents guide interaction points with appetite-stimulating energy.
- Soft pill shapes, warm ambient shadows tinted with burnt umber, and frosted glass navigation panels create depth without corporate detachment.
- Crisp status badges and numeric indicators use culinary herbal greens to reinforce profitability, order throughput, and operational health.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-conversion SaaS patterns in organic, restaurant-inspired undertones.

- **Primary (`#E0533C` - Terracotta Ember):** The core CTA engine. Used for primary conversion triggers, active interactive tabs, key micro-interactions, and high-priority states.
- **Secondary (`#F26A36` - Sunlit Amber):** Accompanying gradient ramp and energetic focus driver. Applied to highlights, promotional chips, and secondary decorative visual accents.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A` - Fresh Basil):** Metric callouts, revenue-gain charts (`+32%`), live status badges, allergen-safe verifications, and zero-fee claims.
- **Neutral (`#1E1B18` - Espresso Roast):** Replaces harsh standard blacks. Forms the text hierarchy foundation and structural framing, delivering contrast without clinical sterility.
- **Surface Foundations:** Warm culinary parchment (`#FAF7F2`) serves as the base layer, shifting to crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, and frosted porcelain (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) for sticky nav bars and floating toolbars.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the unified font family across display, body, and micro-labels. Its open apertures, geometric construction, and friendly rounded terminals mirror culinary openness while maintaining strict legible clarity on mobile displays.

- **Headlines:** Set in extra-bold and bold weights with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to create high-impact, punchy titles that drive conversion.
- **Body:** Kept at generous line-heights (`1.45` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless scannability of features, dish descriptions, and pricing tiers.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Rendered with distinct weights (`600`-`700`) and slight positive tracking for pill chips, allergen tags, pricing badges, and ticket-boost percentage calls.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a mobile-first philosophy that scales into a desktop product showcase:
- **Mobile (Base to 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Content is strictly vertically stacked or housed in horizontal edge-to-edge carousels (e.g., dish card samples, testimonial quotes).
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins, enabling side-by-side interactive mockups and dual-tier pricing matrices.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1200px` with `3rem` outer margins. Hero and demo sections utilize an asymmetric 7/5 split, placing the phone viewport mockup adjacent to value props.

Spacing maintains an 8pt rhythmic progression (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px), guaranteeing comfortable tap targets (minimum 48px) across touchscreens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm atmospheric depth rather than cold, neutral shadows:

- **Level 1 (Card & Dish Elements):** Subtle elevation with a warm espresso-tinted drop: `0px 2px 8px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Phone & Modals):** Medium lift featuring a layered dual shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTA Bar & Toolbars):** High-prominence surface with heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), a frosted fill of `rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.88)`, and a subtle top border highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Level 4 (Popovers & Live QR Triggers):** High-focus floating elevation: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2** (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for cards/containers, and `1.5rem` for prominent modules). Full-pill radii (`rounded-full`) are reserved for chips, badges, buttons, and avatar markers to reinforce friendliness and tactile appeal.

Dish image wrappers, mock phone bezels, and modal dialogs use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px), producing a soft, approachable frame that compliments culinary presentation.

## Components

### Buttons & Sticky Floating CTA
- **Primary Button:** Gradient surface from `#E0533C` to `#F26A36`, white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Elevated by a warm amber aura shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.38)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined or soft tinted button (`rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.08)` surface with `#E0533C` label and `1.5px` border).
- **Mobile Sticky Action Dock:** Fixed bottom dock containing the main conversion trigger ("Empezar Gratis" / "Escanear Demo"), flanked by a live social proof micro-counter ("+1,200 restaurantes"). Encased in frosted parchment blur.

### Dish Cards & Menu Preview Items
- Structured with crisp white containers on parchment surfaces. Top half features a 4:3 high-resolution food visual with corner-nested dietary badges (`Veggie`, `Sin Gluten`).
- Bottom half provides high-contrast typography, item descriptions truncated to 2 lines, price highlighted in deep espresso bold, and an instant increment button (`+`).

### Allergen & Dietary Filter Chips
- Fully rounded pills with a light cream surface (`#F2ECE4`) and dark espresso labels.
- Active state switches instantly to solid espresso (`#1E1B18`) or vibrant terracotta (`#E0533C`) with crisp white iconography.

### Interactive Phone Mockup Container
- Slender device silhouette with frosted glass simulated notch and realistic glass reflections. Houses a live-switchable interactive preview (e.g., Table 4 view, active ordering cart, allergen filter switch).

### Stat & Proof Highlights
- High-visibility container boxes utilizing basil green (`#16A34A`) badge pills (`+32% ticket`, `0% comisiones`) alongside bold espresso metric figures and subtle muted subtext (`#78716A`).

### Pricing Tiers
- Distinct cards with a featured tier highlighted via a 2px border in Terracotta Ember, crowned by an overlapping pill badge reading *"Más Popular"*. Includes clear toggle mechanics between monthly and annual billing cycles.
